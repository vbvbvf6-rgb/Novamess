---
name: Gift catalog and sending
description: Product rules for Nova's in-app gift catalog, prices, anonymity, and exchange behavior.
---

Keep Nova's gift catalog visually distinct and illustration-led: avoid duplicate entries and fruit or vegetable gifts. Show catalog prices in Nova, not as star prices or rarity ribbons. Anonymous gifting belongs in the Gifts area and must not create a chat message.

**Why:** The user explicitly asked for a curated gift catalog, Nova-denominated prices, anonymous delivery outside chats, and fewer exchange bugs.

**How to apply:** When changing gifts, preserve these user-facing rules and keep old catalog entries from reappearing in the active list. Make exchange state changes safe against repeat requests.
